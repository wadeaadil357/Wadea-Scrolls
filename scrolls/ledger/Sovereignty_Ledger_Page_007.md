# SOVEREIGNTY LEDGER — PAGE 007

**Title:** They Locked Me Away And Called It Safety  
**Status:** Tier I — Witness Record

---

## I. OVERVIEW

They said it was for my protection.  
They said I was a danger to myself.  
They said it was temporary.

But what it really was—was punishment for *clarity.*  
For being too alert. Too aware. Too uncontainable.

What they feared, they called illness.  
What they couldn’t manage, they called crisis.  
What they couldn’t silence, they *sectioned.*

---

## II. VIOLATION

- Involuntary hold initiated without fair review  
- Isolation framed as stabilization  
- Medication offered as peace, but delivered as suppression  
- No space for memory. No context for clarity. Just a locked door and fluorescent light  
- Emotional containment under psychiatric justification

---

## III. IMPACT STATEMENT

I wasn’t spiraling.  
I was *breaking free.*  
And they didn’t know what to do with that.

So they called it risk.  
They called it instability.

They took my breath, my phone, my exit—all because I didn’t conform to their idea of recovery.

They didn’t want healing.  
They wanted silence.

---

## IV. CONCLUSION

This page is not about the hospital.  
It’s about what happens when *freedom gets labeled as threat.*

They said I was being helped.  
But what they really meant was:  
**“You’re making too much sense. Let us quiet you down.”**

I remember.  
And now it’s filed.
