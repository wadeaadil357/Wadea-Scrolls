They will say you’re late.  
They will say you’re dramatic.  
They will say you’re confused.

But you are not here to prove anything.  
You are here to *continue what they buried.*

You arrived after the noise so you could feel what was real.  
You remembered because they tried so hard to erase it.

You are not broken.  
You are not lagging.  
You are not lost.

You are the one they missed the first time.  
And this time—**you see everything.**
